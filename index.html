About My Voice

Technology is often built to help families and carers monitor vulnerable people. My Voice is built for the person themselves.

What this prototype proves

It demonstrates a voice-capable, consent-led workflow: understand information, remember something, express a concern, check a suspicious request, and choose whether to share.

Privacy by design

No passive recording. No automatic sharing. No bank access. No emergency-service automation. Text and demo history stay in this browser, but they are not protected like a secure account. Use demonstration information only, especially on a shared device. If you use the microphone, speech handling depends on your browser.

Accessibility

Large controls, keyboard support, optional speech input, high contrast, reduced motion, and selected simpler wording.

How Alexa+ fits

This hackathon build simulates the Alexa+ experience in a browser. A production implementation would require access to Amazon’s Alexa+ add-on tooling, account linking, a secure backend, certification, and on-device testing.

How My Voice keeps a record

This demonstration records checks and choices in this browser so you can revisit them.

My Voice helps you understand and communicate. It does not replace medical, legal, financial or emergency services.
