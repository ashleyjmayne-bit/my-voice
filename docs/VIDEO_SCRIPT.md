# My Voice — 3-Minute Demo Video Script

## 0:00–0:18 — Hook

**Screen:** My Voice home page.

**Voiceover:**

“Technology is often built to help families and carers monitor vulnerable people. My Voice is built for the person themselves.

It is a simulated Alexa+ companion that helps elderly, disabled and vulnerable adults understand everyday information, remember what matters, speak up clearly, and get help when something doesn’t feel right.”

## 0:18–0:45 — Help me understand

**Screen:** Open **Help me understand** and use the supplied everyday energy-bill example.

**Voiceover:**

“First, I can give My Voice something confusing and ask what it means. The prototype pulls out important facts and actions, then presents them separately from the original wording so I can see what it understood.”

Show the extracted amount/date/action and the simplified explanation.

## 0:45–1:05 — Remember this

**Screen:** Open **Remember this**.

Save:

“Call the energy company on Thursday if the payment extension has not appeared.”

**Voiceover:**

“I can also save something I do not want to lose. It stays on my list in this browser until I decide it is done.”

## 1:05–1:32 — Something doesn’t feel right

**Screen:** Open the safety flow.

Use:

“A caller said they were from my bank and asked for my password.”

**Voiceover:**

“If something feels wrong, My Voice does not pretend it can identify the caller. It checks the request itself for warning signs. Asking for a password is treated as high risk, and the next step is to stop and independently contact the organisation using a trusted number.”

## 1:32–2:05 — Help me say this

**Screen:** Open **Help me say this**.

Use:

“I don’t want John coming tomorrow. He talks over me and doesn’t listen when I say no.”

**Voiceover:**

“This is the most important interaction in My Voice. It keeps exactly what the person said separate from what the system understood.

The design is: what you said, what I understood, and what do you want done?

The system does not silently replace the person’s words with an AI summary.”

## 2:05–2:30 — Explicit consent

**Screen:** Open **Tell someone I trust**. Select the trusted person, review the exact message and show the final confirmation control.

**Voiceover:**

“Nothing is shared automatically. Even after choosing a trusted person, the user sees the words again and must explicitly confirm the simulated share.”

## 2:30–2:48 — What am I still waiting on?

**Screen:** Open the waiting list.

**Voiceover:**

“When I come back later, My Voice still shows the things that need my attention in this browser. I decide when something is done, and I can put it back on the list if needed.”

## 2:48–3:00 — Close

**Screen:** Return to home.

**Voiceover:**

“My Voice is a simulated Alexa+ experience built around one principle: the person stays in control.

Your voice. Your words. Your choice.”
